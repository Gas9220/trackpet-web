Features

Everything the vet asks about, a tap away.

Every record your pets need, for as many pets as you have: dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, fish, turtles and more.

All their records

Each pet's page shows its latest weight, temperature, diet, therapy, vaccination, antiparasitic, appointment and bath at a glance, with allergies where they can't be missed.

Weights and temperatures

A chart of every weighing, with the change, the lowest and the highest, and how far the pet is from its target. Temperatures say whether they're normal for the species.

Vaccinations and antiparasitics

Boosters still to give, the soonest first, and how many days of coverage each antiparasitic has left.

Therapies and doses

Each medicine with its dose and the times it's given. Mark each dose given, and see how many have been given so far.

Reminders

At each dose's time, before an appointment, and the day before a booster is due or a coverage ends. Mark a dose given right from the notification.

Calendar

Every pet's records on a month, a dot for each kind, and what happened or is planned on any day.

Documents

Lab results, prescriptions, certificates and invoices, from Photos or Files, or scanned with the camera. Searchable, a section for each year.

Health report

A PDF of a pet's details and records, for its vet or whoever looks after it, to share, print or mark up.

Vet clinics

Your clinics with their phone, email, address and vets, shared by your pets. Tap to call, write or find one in Maps.

Costs

What each visit, vaccine, medicine, antiparasitic and bath cost, a year at a time, for one pet or all of them together.

Siri and Shortcuts

“Log Paco's weight in Trackpet”, or ask what's due today or when the next appointment is. In Spotlight and with the Action button too.

iPad and Mac

Your pets in a sidebar, with the calendar and each pet's page beside them. On the Mac, menus, keyboard shortcuts and what's due today in the menu bar.

Your data, your way

Back up every pet, with its photo and records, to the Files app and restore it, or export spreadsheets in CSV.

Make it yours

Five app icons, kilograms or pounds, Celsius or Fahrenheit, and the app in English, Italiano, Français or Español, whatever your device's language.

For everyone

VoiceOver throughout, and layouts that hold up at the largest text sizes, in light and dark mode.

Mac

At home on the Mac.

A real Mac app, with the sidebar, menus and keyboard shortcuts you expect, and what's due today a click away in the menu bar.

  • What's due today in the menu bar, where you can mark a dose given, even with the window closed

When can I download Trackpet?

It's almost ready: a beta on TestFlight comes first, then the App Store. This page will link to it as soon as it's out.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Which pets can I add?

As many as you like: dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, fish, turtles and other reptiles, and any other animal as Other. Each has its own records, and the calendar shows them all together.

How do I give my vet my pet's records?

On your pet's page, tap Health Report: Trackpet makes a PDF of its details and records and opens it, so you can see it before sharing, printing or saving it. If the pet has a clinic with an email, Send Health Report writes to it with the report attached.

How do I move my pets to a new iPhone?

In Settings › Backup, choose Back Up to Files and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new iPhone, like iCloud Drive. Then, on the new iPhone, choose Restore from Backup. The backup has every pet, with its photo, records and documents.
