Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Trackpet doesn't collect, keep or share any of your data. What you keep about your pets stays on your devices.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and the app doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. If you've chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Your pets' records stay on your devices

Your pets, their records, photos, documents and costs, your vet clinics and your settings are stored only on your device. They don't go through a server.

Photos, camera and documents

Trackpet uses the camera only when you take a pet's photo or scan a document, with your permission, and sees only the photos and files you pick. A photo is made smaller and its metadata, like where it was taken, is removed before it's saved.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device, which shows them at their time. They don't come from a server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Trackpet with Siri, Shortcuts or Spotlight, Apple handles your request, under Apple's privacy policy. Trackpet doesn't receive anything more. Your pets' names are added to your device's Spotlight index, so you can find them there.

Backups, spreadsheets and reports

Backups, CSV spreadsheets and health reports are files you create, saved or shared where you choose, like the Files app, iCloud Drive or an email. Trackpet doesn't send them anywhere.

Emails to your clinic

When you send a health report or a document to a clinic, Trackpet writes the email in your mail app, with the file attached, and you send it yourself. Nothing is sent without you.

Emails to support

If you write to support from the app (Settings › Help and Feedback › Send Feedback), the email includes the app's version, your device model and system version, your language, region and time zone, whether notifications are allowed, and how many pets you have. It never includes your pets' names, notes or photos, and you see the whole email before you send it. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems.

Children

Trackpet doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version, with its date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Write to us: support@example.com
