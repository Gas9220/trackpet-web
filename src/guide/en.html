Getting started

Tap + (or Add Pet on the empty list) to add a pet. Give it a name and a photo, from your library or the camera, and choose its kind: dog, cat, rabbit, bird, fish, turtle, reptile or Other. Everything else is optional, and you can fill it in later with Edit Pet:

  • The breed, the sex, whether it's spayed or neutered, and its date of birth, from which the app works out its age.
  • Its allergies, shown in red at the top of its page so they can't be missed.
  • The microchip, and the insurance with its policy.
  • Its clinic, picked from your vet clinics or added there.
  • For a cat, its FIV and FeLV results: not tested, negative or positive.

The first time you open the app, Try an Example Pet adds Paco, a dog with records of every kind, to look around with. Delete him like any other pet when you're done.

The list's Sort menu orders your pets by Name, Age, Date Added or Last Edited. Swipe a pet, or touch and hold it, to edit or delete it: the app asks first, since deleting a pet deletes all its records and documents.

A pet's page

Tap a pet to open its page. Under its photo, Records has a row for each kind, with the latest at a glance: the last weight and when it was taken, what it eats, the last temperature, the therapy under way and its day, a booster that's due, the antiparasitic's days of coverage left, the next appointment and the last bath. Something that needs attention is in color: a booster overdue or a coverage ended in red, one coming up in orange.

Tap a row to see every record of that kind, and + there to add one. Tap a record to edit it, or swipe it to delete it. Below come Documents and Costs, then the pet's details and its Clinic, whose phone, email and address you tap to call, write or find in Maps.

Weights and temperatures

Weights lists every weighing, the latest first, each with its change since the one before. From two weighings on, a chart shows them over the period you pick, with the change, the lowest and the highest; touch the chart to read a weighing. Under it, Set a Target Weight to see how much the pet has to lose or gain to reach it.

Temperatures are taken with their time of day. Each says whether it's normal for the species: High in red, Low in blue. When you add one, the editor shows the normal range and the last one taken.

Weights are in kilograms or pounds and temperatures in Celsius or Fahrenheit, as you choose in Settings.

Diet and baths

Diet shows what the pet eats Now: the food, its kind (Dry, Wet, Mixed, Raw or Homemade), how much a day in how many meals, and since when. A new diet starts as the current one, so changing the amount or the meals is quick, and what it ate before is kept under Before.

Baths are kept with their day and place, Home or Washing Center, and the list says how often the pet is bathed. A new bath is where the last one was.

Vaccinations and antiparasitics

A vaccination has its vaccine and day, and a Booster with its date if it needs one. Vaccinations lists the boosters still to give first, the soonest first, then every vaccination. Turn on Remind Me the Day Before for a notification at 9:00 the day before the booster is due. A vaccine given before is a tap away when you add the next one.

An antiparasitic is a Collar, a Spot-On or a Pill, with its product, the day it was applied and the day its coverage ends. Active Coverage shows the days left, in orange in the last ones. A new one starts as the last one was, so a monthly drop or an 8-month collar is one tap. Its reminder comes the day before the coverage ends.

Therapies and doses

A therapy has its reason, like an ear infection, and its medicines. Add the therapy, then its medicines from its page: each with its dose, its first and last day, and the times it's given. The therapy's days come from its medicines, and its page shows how far along it is.

  • Today, on the therapy's page, has a button for each of today's dose times: tap it to mark the dose given, or again to take it back.
  • Each medicine says how many doses have been given so far, out of those due.
  • With reminders on, a notification comes at each dose time, every day of the medicine. Its Given button marks the dose given without opening the app.
  • Used Before gives a medicine again with its dose, for as many days.

Appointments and reminders

An appointment has its reason, its date and time, and a place, which can be the pet's clinic in a tap. Remind Me sends a notification 1 Hour Before, 1 Day Before or 2 Days Before, and Add to Calendar puts it in your calendar too. Appointments lists the upcoming ones, the soonest first, then the past ones.

The app asks to send notifications only when you first turn a reminder on. Tapping a reminder opens its record. Settings › Notifications shows whether they're allowed, and opens the system's settings if they're off.

Calendar

The Calendar tab (on iPad and Mac, the first row of the sidebar) shows every pet's records on a month, with a dot under each day for each kind of record on it. Tap a day to see everything on it for all your pets: weighings, temperatures, vaccinations, baths and appointments, a new diet, the boosters and antiparasitics due, and the days of a therapy. Tap one to open it. Today brings you back to today, and the filter menu shows All Records or one kind.

Documents

Keep a pet's Lab Results, Prescriptions, Certificates, Vaccination Booklet and Invoices with its records. Add one with Scan Document, which turns the pages you scan with the camera into one PDF, Choose Photo or Choose File, or drop a file on the list on iPad and Mac. Give it a title, a kind, a date and notes.

The documents are in a section for each year, the latest first. Search them by title, kind, note, clinic, vet or year. Tap one to open it in Quick Look, where you can share, print or mark it up.

The health report and your clinics

On a pet's page, Health Report makes a PDF of its details and records: allergies, microchip, clinic and insurance, weights with their chart, temperatures, diet, vaccinations and boosters, antiparasitics, therapies, appointments, baths and documents. It opens in Quick Look, so you see it before you share, print or save it.

Your clinics are in Settings › Vet Clinics, each with its phone, email, address and vets, and shared by your pets. A clinic's page shows its contacts, its vets with the last day each saw a pet, and its pets. Vaccinations, appointments, therapies and documents can each name a clinic and its vet.

When the pet's clinic has an email, Send Health Report in the page's Clinic section, or Send to the clinic in a document's menu, writes an email to it with the file attached. You send it yourself.

Costs

A visit, a vaccine, a medicine, an antiparasitic and a bath can each have a cost, in its editor. A pet's Costs show a year at a time: the total, a bar for each month, each kind's total, and every cost, each opening its record. Switch to all your pets together to see what each one cost.

Siri, Shortcuts and the Home Screen

Say “Log Paco's weight in Trackpet”, or log a temperature or a bath, and ask Siri What's Due or when the Next Appointment is, for one pet or all of them. These work in Spotlight, the Shortcuts app and with the Action button too, without setting anything up.

  • Your pets are in Spotlight: search for a pet's name, kind or breed to open its page.
  • Touch and hold the app's icon for New Pet and your first three pets.

iPad and Mac

On iPad and Mac your pets are in a sidebar, with the calendar as its first row and the page you pick beside it. With a keyboard, ⌘N adds a pet and ⌘, opens Settings.

  • The Mac's menus have New Pet (⌘N), Settings… (⌘,) and Calendar (⌘0).
  • A paw in the menu bar shows what's due today for each pet, with the day's doses to mark given, even with the window closed. Turn it off in Settings › Menu Bar.

Settings and your data

Tap the gear at the top of the list for Settings:

  • Language: the device's own, or English, Italiano, Français or Español for the app alone.
  • Weight, Temperature and Appearance (System, Light or Dark), and the App Icon, one of five.
  • Backup: Back Up to Files saves every pet, with its photo, records and documents, in one file, and Restore from Backup brings them back, replacing the pets on the device. Pets aren't synced between devices yet, so a backup is how you keep them safe, and how you move them to a new iPhone. The app reminds you if you haven't backed up in a while.
  • Spreadsheets: Export as CSV shares your pets and each kind of record as spreadsheets, for Numbers or Excel.
  • Help and Feedback: Send Feedback writes to us with details that help find a problem, never your pets' names, notes or photos.
